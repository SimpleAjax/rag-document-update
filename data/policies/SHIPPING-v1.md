# Standard delivery

Northstar Devices normally dispatches an in-stock device within two business days after payment clears. Standard delivery usually takes three to five business days after dispatch. A carrier delay can change the delivery date.

# Tracking updates

Northstar Devices sends a tracking link after the carrier accepts the parcel. The tracking page can take one business day to show its first scan. The customer can contact support if the page has no scan after that time.

# Address changes

The customer can request an address change before the carrier accepts the parcel. Support must confirm the order and the new address before it makes the change. Northstar Devices cannot promise a change after the first carrier scan.

# Delivery attempts

The carrier can leave the parcel at the delivery address or ask for a signature. The carrier decides which option it uses. If nobody can accept the parcel, the carrier can make another attempt or hold it at a local pickup point.

# Damaged parcels

The customer must report visible parcel damage to support within two calendar days after delivery. The customer should keep the packaging and provide photographs when available. Support records the report and explains the next review step.

# Remote locations

Some remote locations need extra delivery time. The checkout page shows an estimate when the carrier provides one. The estimate is not a guaranteed delivery date.

# Split shipments

Northstar Devices can send items from one order in separate parcels. Each parcel has its own tracking link. The order page shows which items belong to each parcel and whether another item is still waiting for dispatch.
